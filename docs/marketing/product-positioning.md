# Product Positioning

## Ideal Customer Profile

Esteo's initial ICP is a small service business that prepares estimates manually and wants faster, more consistent client responses.

Primary ICP:

- renovation companies,
- construction and finishing teams,
- small trade/service companies,
- owner-led teams where the same person sells, estimates, and manages work,
- teams that currently use Excel, copied Word/PDF files, email threads, and manual calculations.

Common traits:

- estimates are frequent but time-consuming,
- client requests arrive with incomplete details,
- pricing depends on scope and manual judgment,
- PDF presentation matters,
- speed of response affects conversion,
- the team is not ready for complex enterprise software.

## Core Problems

Esteo solves these problems:

- Starting every estimate from a blank file takes too long.
- Customer requests are unstructured and incomplete.
- Estimate formatting and PDF creation are repetitive.
- Scope, margins, quantities, and sections are easy to lose across copied files.
- Manual workflows make it hard to respond quickly.
- Existing tools are either too generic, too manual, or too complex.
- AI chat alone is not enough because estimates still need structure and review.

## Alternatives

### Excel Or Google Sheets

Strengths:

- flexible,
- familiar,
- cheap,
- easy to start.

Weaknesses:

- no request-to-estimate workflow,
- no built-in AI draft pipeline,
- manual PDF formatting,
- easy to duplicate mistakes,
- weak branding,
- no guided customer intake,
- limited collaboration and audit trail.

Esteo message:

> Keep the control you like in spreadsheets, but get a workflow built for estimates.

### Copied Word Or PDF Templates

Strengths:

- familiar presentation,
- easy to reuse.

Weaknesses:

- hard to maintain,
- no structured calculations,
- slow edits,
- no AI-assisted draft creation,
- inconsistent formatting.

Esteo message:

> Stop copying old files. Start from a structured draft and export a professional PDF.

### Generic CRM Or Project Management Tools

Strengths:

- customer pipeline,
- tasks,
- broader business workflow.

Weaknesses:

- estimating is not the core workflow,
- line items and PDFs often require add-ons or manual setup,
- AI rarely understands estimate structure.

Esteo message:

> Esteo focuses on the estimating workflow first.

### AI Chat Tools

Strengths:

- fast ideation,
- flexible text generation.

Weaknesses:

- no workspace context,
- no controlled estimate table,
- no PDF pipeline,
- no usage limits tied to business plans,
- no review/approval workflow,
- easy to produce unverified numbers.

Esteo message:

> Use AI inside an estimating workflow, not in a blank chat window.

## Why Choose Esteo

Reasons to choose Esteo:

1. It turns project details into an editable estimate draft.
2. AI assists, but the user stays responsible for scope and pricing.
3. It produces branded, client-ready PDFs.
4. It connects public requests, internal creation, voice intake, drafts, versions, and PDFs.
5. It is designed for practical service teams, not generic enterprise workflows.

## Key Differentiators

### Request-To-Estimate Workflow

Customer and internal requests become structured estimate drafts through one pipeline.

### Human-In-The-Loop AI

AI drafts and suggests changes, but users approve edits before sending anything to clients.

### Professional PDF Output

PDF export is part of the product workflow, not an afterthought.

### Voice Intake

Customers and workspace users can describe a project by voice and fill the form faster.

### Workspace Branding

Company profile and branding can shape estimates and PDFs, making the output feel professional.

## Messaging To Repeat

Use these messages across Landing, Pricing, FAQ, Security, and future Demo/Templates:

- "Create professional estimates faster."
- "AI prepares a draft. You review and approve."
- "Turn project details into a structured estimate."
- "Send client-ready PDFs with your company branding."
- "Built for service teams that need speed, clarity, and control."

## Positioning Statement

For service companies that still prepare estimates manually, Esteo is an AI-assisted estimating workspace that turns customer details into structured drafts and professional PDFs, while keeping the user in control of scope and pricing.

## First Landing Angle

The launch landing should not start with "AI". It should start with the business pain:

> Preparing estimates takes too long when every request starts as notes, calls, photos, and copied spreadsheet rows.

Then introduce Esteo:

> Esteo turns those details into a structured draft you can review, edit, and export as a professional PDF.

AI should be framed as the accelerator inside the workflow, not as the product itself.
