# Feature Marketing Map

## Purpose

This matrix maps Esteo product capabilities to marketing surfaces, assets, and SEO opportunities. It prevents the landing page, pricing page, FAQ, and future content from telling different stories about the product.

## Legend

- `Launch`: include in Launch MVP.
- `Later`: keep for a later marketing phase.
- `No`: do not emphasize on this surface.
- `Proof needed`: requires screenshot, video, or stronger product evidence before promotion.

## Launch MVP Feature Narrative

Launch MVP should consistently communicate this sequence:

1. Capture project details.
2. Generate an AI-assisted draft.
3. Review and edit the estimate table.
4. Export a professional PDF.
5. Stay in control of scope and pricing.

This sequence should appear in:

- Landing hero/workflow,
- FAQ,
- AI Disclaimer,
- Security trust copy,
- future demo/video.

## Feature Messaging Details

### AI-Assisted Estimate Drafts

Marketing angle:

- "Start from a structured draft instead of a blank spreadsheet."
- "AI helps prepare the estimate, you review the final numbers."

Use on:

- Landing hero,
- workflow section,
- FAQ,
- AI Disclaimer,
- SEO content.

Avoid:

- "AI creates final estimates automatically."
- "No review needed."
- "Guaranteed accurate pricing."

### Human Review And Approval

Marketing angle:

- "You stay in control."
- "Approve changes before they affect the estimate."

Use on:

- Landing AI section,
- FAQ,
- legal AI copy,
- security/trust band.

### Editable Estimate Table

Marketing angle:

- "Structured, editable estimates with sections and line items."
- "Change scope, quantities, margins, and details before sending."

Use on:

- screenshot section,
- workflow section,
- feature cards.

### PDF Export

Marketing angle:

- "Send a branded PDF your client can review."
- "Move from draft to client-ready document."

Use on:

- Landing,
- future templates,
- SEO content around `kosztorys PDF`.

### Public Estimate Request Form

Marketing angle:

- "Collect project details in a structured way."
- "Use one flow for customer requests and internal estimates."

Use on:

- workflow,
- screenshot,
- FAQ.

### Voice Intake

Marketing angle:

- "Describe the project by voice and fill the request faster."

Use carefully:

- Landing feature card,
- FAQ,
- future demo.

Do not make voice the main launch headline. It is a strong differentiator, but the main product promise is faster professional estimates.

### Workspace Branding

Marketing angle:

- "Keep estimates consistent with your company profile."
- "Professional output without manual formatting."

Use on:

- PDF and trust sections,
- future feature pages.

### Billing Plans And Usage Limits

Marketing angle:

- "Start with a plan that fits your usage."
- "Upgrade when estimate volume and team needs grow."

Use on:

- Pricing,
- FAQ.

Avoid on:

- hero section,
- early landing sections.

## Rules For Adding New Marketing Claims

Before adding a feature claim to a public page, check:

- Is the feature implemented and stable?
- Does it matter to the ICP?
- Is there a screenshot, video, or clear proof point?
- Does the message match Brand Strategy?
- Does it avoid overpromising AI output?
- Is it covered in FAQ or legal copy if it may create risk?

## Acceptance Criteria

- Launch MVP features are clearly separated from future features.
- Every homepage feature card maps to a real product capability.
- Every promoted AI claim is paired with human-control language.
- Asset requirements are identified before implementation.
- SEO opportunities are assigned to feature clusters.
