# Brand Strategy

## Mission

Help service companies prepare clear, professional estimates faster, without losing control over scope, pricing, or client communication.

Esteo exists to reduce the operational friction around estimating work: collecting project details, turning them into a structured draft, reviewing the numbers, and sending a polished PDF to the client.

## Vision

Esteo should become the operating layer for estimate-heavy service businesses: a place where requests, AI-assisted drafts, company rules, pricing, PDFs, and customer communication come together in one workflow.

The product should feel like professional business software that understands how small service teams actually work, not like a generic AI toy.

## Tone Of Voice

Esteo should sound:

- clear,
- practical,
- calm,
- professional,
- specific,
- trustworthy,
- helpful without overpromising.

Avoid:

- hype-heavy AI language,
- generic startup slogans,
- vague productivity claims,
- claims that AI replaces expert judgment,
- playful or childish wording,
- fear-based sales copy.

Preferred language:

- "Create a draft faster."
- "Review before sending."
- "Keep your pricing under control."
- "Turn project details into a structured estimate."
- "Professional PDFs ready for clients."

Avoid:

- "AI does everything for you."
- "Never make mistakes again."
- "Revolutionary estimating."
- "Magical automation."

## Brand Personality

Esteo is:

- Premium, but not luxury.
- Technical, but not cold.
- Calm, but not boring.
- AI-assisted, but human-controlled.
- Operational, but still polished.
- Trustworthy, but not corporate-heavy.

Reference feel:

- Linear for clarity and precision.
- Notion for calm structure.
- Stripe for trust and polish.
- Modern architecture software for professional utility.

Esteo should not feel like:

- a colorful startup template,
- a consumer AI chatbot,
- a construction clipart website,
- an enterprise procurement portal.

## Value Proposition

Esteo helps service companies turn project details into professional, reviewable estimates faster, with AI assistance, structured workflows, and client-ready PDFs.

Short version:

> Professional estimates, faster, with AI-assisted drafts you stay in control of.

## USP

Esteo is differentiated by the combination of:

- AI-assisted estimate drafts from customer or internal requests.
- Human-in-the-loop editing and approval.
- Structured estimate tables instead of unstructured documents.
- Client-ready PDF generation.
- Workspace-level branding and company profile.
- Voice intake for faster request capture.
- Billing and usage limits designed around small service teams.

The strongest USP is not "AI estimates". It is "AI-assisted estimating workflow from request to reviewed PDF".

## Target Audience

Primary audience:

- owners of small service companies,
- renovation and construction teams,
- people preparing estimates for clients,
- small operational teams that currently rely on Excel, copied documents, emails, and manual PDF workflows.

Secondary audience:

- growing service companies that need consistent templates, branding, and repeatable estimating workflows,
- teams that receive many inbound requests and want faster first response time.

## Positioning

Esteo is positioned as an AI-assisted estimating workspace for service companies.

It is not:

- just a PDF generator,
- a generic CRM,
- a generic spreadsheet replacement,
- a fully autonomous pricing engine,
- a chatbot-first tool.

Positioning statement:

> For service companies that prepare estimates manually, Esteo is an AI-assisted estimating workspace that turns project details into structured drafts and professional PDFs, while keeping the user in control of scope and pricing.

## Messaging Pillars

### 1. Faster First Draft

Turn customer details, internal notes, or voice input into a structured estimate draft faster than starting from a blank sheet.

### 2. Human Control

AI helps prepare and adjust the draft, but users review, edit, approve, and send the final estimate.

### 3. Professional Output

Generate branded, client-ready PDFs instead of manually formatting documents.

### 4. Repeatable Workflow

Keep requests, drafts, versions, PDFs, company rules, and billing in one workspace.

### 5. Built For Service Teams

Esteo speaks to practical estimating work: scope, quantities, margins, customer requests, PDFs, and response time.

## Messaging Rules

Use consistently:

- "AI-assisted", not "fully automated".
- "Draft", not "final quote" when referring to AI output.
- "Estimate", not "invoice".
- "Review and edit", not "set and forget".
- "Professional PDF", not "document export" when speaking to customers.

Every key marketing page should reinforce:

- faster drafting,
- human control,
- professional output,
- trust and data responsibility.
