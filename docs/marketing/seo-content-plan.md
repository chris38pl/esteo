# SEO Content Plan

## Purpose

This document defines the content side of SEO for Esteo. It complements technical SEO work such as metadata, sitemap, robots, OpenGraph, and structured data.

The goal is to decide what search demand Esteo should target and which pages should answer each intent.

## Scope

Launch MVP focuses on Polish keywords first. English keywords should be added after the Polish positioning and content structure are validated.

Launch MVP pages:

- Landing
- Pricing
- FAQ
- Contact
- Security
- Privacy
- Terms
- Cookies
- AI Disclaimer

Future SEO pages:

- Templates
- Demo
- Changelog
- Roadmap
- Blog
- Docs
- Integrations
- Feature pages
- Case studies

## Keyword Clusters

### Estimating Software

Primary phrases:

- `program do kosztorysów`
- `program do wycen usług`
- `program dla ekip remontowych`
- `software dla firm budowlanych`
- `program do kosztorysów remontowych`

Intent:

- commercial investigation,
- software comparison,
- purchase or trial readiness.

Best pages:

- Landing,
- Pricing,
- future Feature pages.

Content angle:

- professional estimates,
- faster workflow,
- AI-assisted draft,
- PDF output,

## Launch MVP SEO Requirements

Landing:

- target one broad commercial phrase cluster,
- include copy for AI-assisted estimates,
- include PDF and workflow language,
- avoid overloading page with too many keyword variants.

Pricing:

- include clear plan names and limits,
- answer billing, cancellation, and workspace ownership questions,
- link to Terms.

FAQ:

- answer AI, accuracy, PDF, pricing, data, cancellation, and support questions,
- structure questions so they can later become FAQ JSON-LD.

Security:

- explain high-level data handling,
- link to Privacy, Cookies, AI Disclaimer,
- avoid unsupported security claims.

Legal:

- should be indexable unless legal review says otherwise,
- use clear titles and canonical URLs.

## Content Rules

- Write for business owners and estimators, not developers.
- Do not claim AI guarantees accuracy.
- Keep keywords natural.
- Prefer service-business language over generic SaaS language.
- Use Polish phrases first; translate intent, not literal keywords, for English.

## Open Research Tasks

Before implementation, validate:

- search volume,
- keyword difficulty,
- competitor SERPs,
- whether consumer-intent phrases should be targeted or avoided,
- whether "kosztorys remontu" attracts contractors or homeowners more often.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every Launch MVP page has a clear SEO intent.
- Keyword clusters are assigned to pages.
- Future SEO pages are identified but not required for launch.
- SEO plan avoids forcing blog/templates into Launch MVP.
- Technical SEO work can use this document to define titles, descriptions, headings, and FAQ structure.
