# Headline:

Fast AI-powered estimates for your services

# Taglines

- Create professional estimates in minutes with AI.

- Faster quotes. More clients. Less chaos.
- Esteo helps service companies create professional estimates in minutes using AI.

# Who is it for?

- Small and medium-sized service businesses creating estimates manually
- Renovation crews and installers preparing 5–50 estimates per month

# ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

- A small home renovation company that creates quotes manually in Excel or other tools and lacks a professional estimate document they can send to clients.

# Initial target industries

- Interior finishing / renovations
- Electricians
- Carpenters
- Plumbers
- Installers

# What problem does it solve?

- Creating estimates takes too much time
- Companies respond too slowly to leads
- Low transparency for customers
- Estimates are chaotic
- Owners copy old PDFs
- Pricing is inconsistent
- Difficult to calculate labor and materials
- Quotes look unprofessional
- Estimates are created after working hours
- Small businesses lose customers because of slow replies

# How does it work?

- The user provides:
- Investment / project / estimate description (free text)
- Photos or PDF project files
- Contact and address details of the investment/project
- Industry-specific details (e.g. area size, preferred start date, property type)

# What does AI do?

- Helps the potential customer complete the estimate form
- AI assistant checks what additional information should be added
- Helps the service provider automatically generate an estimate draft
- Detects work scope
- Splits work into stages
- Calculates materials
- Estimates labor hours
- Suggests prices (or uses uploaded pricing lists)

# What is the result?

The contractor receives a professional estimate that can be further edited with AI assistance
The estimate is divided into configurable sections or AI-generated sections depending on the project type
The contractor can generate a PDF or send the estimate via email or shareable URL
The contractor can add comments and manually edit the estimate easily

# Pricing model

## Free

- 3 estimates per month
- PDF watermark
- AI assistant in estimate editor: **3 prompts per calendar month**, only **one estimate** may use the assistant that month
- Undo: **1** last change

## Pro (89 PLN/month)

- Workspace creation and sharing
- 1 workspace limit
- Up to 3 invited users
- Branding
- PDF export
- Unlimited estimate storage
- AI assistant: **10 prompts per estimate**
- Undo: **3** last changes

## Business (199 PLN/month)

- Multiple workspaces
- Unlimited shared users
- Templates
- Pricing catalogs
- Website integration (API/webhook for estimate requests)
- AI assistant: **unlimited** prompts
- Undo: **3** last changes
